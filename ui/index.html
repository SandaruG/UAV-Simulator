MODEL NOTES

Research context

The interface drives the repository's uavlink_sim controller and calls uavlink_eval after runs. Predictive options route through the existing Part 3 policy adapter; reactive and bonding use native simulator policies.

Radio SINR, received power, packet error, routing, blind time, and radio power are simulator telemetry. The simulator policy contract does not expose calibrated per-radio latency or independent video-and-joint outage forecasts; these are marked unavailable rather than estimated.

Scenario buildings and terrain are synthetic or scenario-defined. They are research-model visualizations, not calibrated real-world maps. Batch comparisons use the same trial seed for each policy and should be interpreted with the evaluation report and its sample size.
